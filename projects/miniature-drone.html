Miniature Drone

A one-day robotics hackathon project where a team of four engineered a miniature drone from supplied parts.

Overview

This project required quick analysis of supplied components and rapid construction of a working drone circuit. I worked with a team to connect motors, wire the system, and build toward a functioning prototype.

Technical Focus

  • Motor wiring
  • Microcontroller connections
  • Circuit assembly
  • Soldering
  • Team-based rapid prototyping

Tools Used

Soldering Motors Microcontroller Circuit Design Robotics

What I Learned

This project helped develop practical circuit-building instincts, especially around connecting motors to a microcontroller under time pressure.
