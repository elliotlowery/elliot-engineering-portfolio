M&M Color Detector & Sorting System

An automated M&M sorting system that detects the color of individual candies and directs them into corresponding removable baskets using a servo-driven sorting chute.

Overview

The system uses a color sensor to identify individual M&Ms and automatically sort them into different baskets. A servo-driven sorting chute rotates between color-coded positions to direct each candy to the appropriate location.

Candy is fed through a large hopper equipped with a spinning selector wheel that stages and releases individual M&Ms onto the chute one at a time.

My Contributions

  • Wired the motors, Arduino, LiPo battery, and color sensor
  • Programmed the Arduino to detect M&M colors
  • Developed the control logic for the sorting mechanism
  • Programmed the servo to rotate the chute to the correct color position
  • Integrated the color sensor and lighting system for consistent readings
